16 November 2026 · This is a readable extract of the downloaded public webpage, not an original vendor PDF brochure. The complete HTML and text are saved alongside it.

Who this course is for
Engineers working full-time inside a company that are prepared to bring a real use case from work and iterate on the AI solution weekly.
If you are a startup founder, consultant, big tech employee, or currently unemployed, this course is likely NOT for you.
If you are unable to get sponsorship from your company to take this course, this course is likely NOT for you.
Prerequisites
Git & Python mastery, Coding Agent (e.g., Claude Code or Codex) proficiency
VSCode with GitHub Copilot (or equivalent) should be your daily driver already.
Experience writing code that is deployed to production software environments
If you have never held a job where you contribute code to a production software environment, this class is not for you.
Machine Learning & Deep Learning fundamentals
This means an understanding of training, testing, and inference for various model architectures, including LLMs
What's included
Live sessions
Learn directly from your instructors in a real-time, interactive format.
Access to world-class experts & personalized feedback
In addition to being hosted by Dr. Greg, you will be taught in our lectures by world-class experts who do this work every day. Within breakout rooms, you will be guided by peer supporters who are on similar journeys to master the role of AI FDE. For each assignment, your peer supporter will provide personalized feedback
The AI FDE Certification
Join hundreds who have created real ROI for themselves and their companies through an AI Makerspace certification
Maven Guarantee
Your purchase is backed by the Maven Guarantee.
