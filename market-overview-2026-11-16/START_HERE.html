What the comparison says

Within this five-provider sample, FDE education combines AI engineering, customer discovery and production delivery. RAG, agents and cloud deployment are already common syllabus elements. The strategic question for Scaler is how convincingly graduates can demonstrate independent customer delivery.

Implication for Scaler

Position the advanced track around assessed delivery: a scoped customer problem, justified architecture, measured AI quality, tested recovery, production handover and individual defence. Several competitors already teach these themes; Scaler must make the graduation standard explicit and demonstrate it consistently.

Longer FDE career pathway · 8 months / 32 weeks

Futurense FDE Academy

A four-phase program combining technical and consulting tracks: foundations, core build, deployment depth, and capstone mastery. Topics progress through RAG, evaluation, reliability, MCP, multi-agent systems and training/serving a small language model.

Learner evidence: The final phase advertises two full projects, five written cases and a live capstone panel.

Lesson for Scaler: Benchmark its parallel consulting track and sustained portfolio development.

The public brochure request requires contact details and an OTP. The attachment is a saved public curriculum, not that brochure.

Advanced workplace upskilling · 20 live sessions / 10 projects

AI Makerspace

The AI FDE Certification targets experienced, employed engineers who bring a workplace use case and company support. It covers open models, retrieval, evaluation, agents, guardrails, memory, adaptation and enterprise operation.

Learner evidence: Weekly project work develops a workplace pilot toward production, with client-facing reporting. The page lists four live hours weekly; project work is additional.

Lesson for Scaler: Benchmark real stakeholder pressure and feedback on an evolving engagement.

This is the AI FDE Certification, not the separate AI Engineering Bootcamp. Cohort and individual lesson dates are inconsistent; confirm the timetable.

Short intensive bootcamp · 12 weeks

K21Academy

An 18-module program advertising 45 labs, 17 checkpoints and one capstone. It combines Python, APIs, SQL and React with RAG, LangGraph, MCP, evaluation, security and cloud delivery. Five modules address customer-facing work.

Learner evidence: Learners build application layers, produce discovery/design artifacts and finish with a deployed capstone and executive demo.

Lesson for Scaler: Benchmark the visible deliverables and frequent checkpoints; validate entry readiness against the breadth of a short course.

Published syllabus breadth does not establish the depth achieved by an individual learner.

Delivery program for people who code · Advertised 26 weeks

Fireblaze AI School

Six phases cover engineering foundations, heterogeneous systems integration, AI applications, production operation, FDE craft and a team capstone. The AI phase includes RAG, agents, evaluation, tracing and model-selection decisions.

Learner evidence: Weekly mock-stakeholder presentations lead to a four-week team engagement, deployed system, handover documentation and panel defence.

Lesson for Scaler: Benchmark messy enterprise integration and repeated practice explaining technical trade-offs.

The six published phase lengths total 24 weeks against a 26-week headline. Confirm how the remaining time is allocated.

Career pathway marketed to the Middle East · Advertised 7 months

Masai + iHUB DivyaSampark

A six-module pathway from programming and SQL through customer-system integration, evaluated LLM applications, controlled agents/MCP, production engineering and a customer-engagement capstone. It advertises 8–10 hours weekly, including study.

Learner evidence: A deployed, defended and handed-over customer solution; certification is from iHUB DivyaSampark, IIT Roorkee. This is a certificate program, not an IIT degree.

Lesson for Scaler: Benchmark explicit customer skills and an engagement carried through the curriculum. An advanced Scaler track can validate foundational skills at admission.

Module allocations total 32 weeks versus the seven-month label. The brochure link is included; the local download failed certificate verification.
